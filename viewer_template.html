Planning aid, not a survey. Mapped lines come from OpenStreetMap (ODbL, fetched 20 Nov 2026). Imagery lines were read by eye from Esri World Imagery and are good to about 20 m. Scout lines are computed from a 30 m elevation model and must be walked and confirmed before any guest uses them. A missing path means unmapped, not absent. The walk-through is a low flight over satellite imagery draped on terrain, not street-level photography. Leave a comment or correction.
